Clinical red-flag screen for deep vein thrombosis (DVT), septic arthritis, neuropathic / Charcot arthropathy, spreading infection, and acute/critical limb ischaemia.

Key points

  1. DVT: screen for unilateral calf swelling, deep venous tenderness, warmth, pitting oedema, and immobilisation/post-op risk -> same-day medical referral.
  2. Septic joint & spreading infection: hot, swollen, erythematous joint with severe restriction of motion, systemic fever/rigors, or diabetic foot ulcer with cellulitis -> same-day emergency referral.
  3. Neuropathic / Charcot foot: warm, swollen, erythematous midfoot/ankle in a patient with peripheral neuropathy (often with minimal pain) -> immediate offloading and specialist referral.

Positive / result

Presence of any vascular, infective, thromboembolic, or neuropathic Charcot red flag -> same-day referral.

Pre-treatment medication and safety check for fluoroquinolone-associated tendonitis/rupture (van der Linden 2003, Wise 2012), anticoagulants before dry needling (Kearns 2019), and electrophysical-agent contraindications (Houghton 2010).

Key points

  1. Screen for current or recent fluoroquinolone antibiotic exposure and concomitant oral glucocorticoids, particularly in patients aged over 60 years.
  2. Screen for anticoagulant therapy or coagulopathy before considering dry needling (caution — possible increased incidence or severity of haematoma and bruising, Kearns 2019).
  3. Screen for electrophysical-agent contraindications and precautions before applying any modality: implanted electronic devices, pregnancy, malignancy, recently irradiated tissue, active DVT, bleeding, and impaired sensation (Houghton 2010).

Clinical decision rules to exclude ankle and midfoot fractures without radiography in adults and children.

Key points

  1. Palpate the posterior edge or tip of the lateral malleolus (distal 6 cm) for bone tenderness.
  2. Palpate the posterior edge or tip of the medial malleolus (distal 6 cm) for bone tenderness.
  3. Palpate the base of the 5th metatarsal and the navicular bone for midfoot bone tenderness.

Standardised hand-held dynamometry (HHD) force and torque (N·m = N × m) measurement for plantarflexion, dorsiflexion, inversion, eversion, and hip abduction.

Key points
