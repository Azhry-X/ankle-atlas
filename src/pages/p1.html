B · Fracture (Ottawa Rules), Achilles Rupture, Compartment Syndrome, Red Flags & Medication / Safety StripTRIAGE GATE
Fracture — Ottawa Ankle & Foot RulesOttawa Calc
Ottawa ankle & foot rules (SR, 27 studies) (Bachmann 2003 BMJ) H: LR− 0.08 (0.03–0.18) ankle and 0.08 (0.03–0.20) mid-foot; children 0.07; < 1.4% probability of fracture at 15% prevalence; radiographs reduced by 30–40% (bone tenderness at posterior edge or tip of distal 6 cm of lateral or medial malleolus, navicular, base of 5th MT, or inability to bear weight four steps)
Ottawa ankle rules — children (SR) (Dowling 2009 Acad Emerg Med) H: pooled sensitivity 98.5% (97.3–99.2); x-ray reduction 24.8%
Achilles Rupture & Compartment SyndromeMaffulli 1998
Achilles rupture — clinical tests & triad (Maffulli 1998 Am J Sports Med) M: calf squeeze (Thompson) sensitivity 0.96, Matles 0.88; at least two tests positive in all patients with a tear → same-day referral
Compartment syndrome (lower leg) (Ulmer 2002 J Orthop Trauma) L: positive predictive value of clinical findings 11% to 15% (pain out of proportion / passive stretch pain → emergent surgical referral)
Red Flags in Words & Medication / Safety StripWise 2012
Same-day red flags E: DVT (calf swelling, warmth, tenderness); septic joint (hot, swollen joint, fever, inability to bear weight); Lisfranc injury (plantar midfoot ecchymosis, TMT instability); neuropathic / Charcot foot (red, hot, swollen foot in neuropathy); infection / spreading cellulitis; ischaemic foot (pulseless, pale, cold, rest pain)
Fluoroquinolones & Achilles rupture (van der Linden 2003 Arch Intern Med · Wise 2012 Am J Med) M: adjusted OR 4.3 current, 2.4 recent exposure; OR 6.4 (60–79 years) and 20.4 (≥ 80 years); 2 % to 6 % of ruptures after 60 attributable to quinolones · tendonitis OR 4.3, rupture OR 2.0; stronger with oral glucocorticoids (OR 9.1) and age > 60 (OR 8.3)
Anticoagulants / coagulopathy before needling (Kearns 2019 J Man Manip Ther) E: caution — possible increased incidence or severity of haematoma and bruising
Electrophysical-agent contraindications (Houghton 2010 Physiother Can) (Guideline): implanted electronic devices, pregnancy, malignancy, recently irradiated tissue, active DVT, bleeding, impaired sensation
TRIAGE DISPOSITION: Cleared — proceed Radiographs required (Ottawa positive) Same-day referral (Achilles rupture / compartment / DVT / septic / Lisfranc / Charcot / ischaemic)
