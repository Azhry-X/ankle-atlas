Achilles Rupture — Operative vs Non-OperativeOchen 2019
Operative vs non-operative (MA, 29 studies) (Ochen 2019 BMJ) H: re-rupture 2.3% vs 3.9% (RR 0.43); complications 4.9% vs 1.6% (RR 2.76); with accelerated functional rehabilitation no significant re-rupture difference (RR 0.60)
Accelerated non-operative protocol (RCT) (Willits 2010 J Bone Joint Surg Am) H: outcomes clinically similar to operative treatment, avoiding surgical complications
RCT meta-analysis (early weight-bearing studies) (Xu 2025 Ann Med) M: fewer re-ruptures with minimally invasive (RR 0.28) and open repair (RR 0.17); open repair complications RR 3.03; minimally invasive sural nerve injury RR 5.86
Lateral Ligament Repair & Osteochondral LesionCho 2025
Broström + suture tape (case series) (Coetzee 2018 Foot Ankle Int) L: mean return to sport 84.1 days; single-leg hop LSI normal / near normal in 86.4%; FAAM Sports ≥ 90 in 79.0%
Modified Broström + suture tape (≥ 3 years, n = 94) (Cho 2025 J Clin Med) L: recurrent instability 2.1%; FAAM 90.5 at final follow-up
Talar osteochondral lesions — bone marrow stimulation (MA) (Luca 2025 Clin Sports Med) L: return to sport 83.3%; to pre-injury level 52.7%
