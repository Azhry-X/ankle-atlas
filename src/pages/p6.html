Shockwave & Corticosteroid Injection EvidenceAqil 2013
Shockwave for chronic plantar fasciitis (MA) (Aqil 2013 Clin Orthop Relat Res) M: VAS reduction SMD 0.60 (0.34–0.85); 60% improvement on first steps RR 1.30
Focused shockwave for plantar fasciitis (MA) (Sun 2017 Medicine (Baltimore)) M: improvement rate OR 2.58 (1.97–3.39) vs placebo
Achilles tendinopathy — eccentric vs shockwave vs wait-and-see (RCT) (Rompe 2007 Am J Sports Med) M: “completely recovered / much improved” 60% vs 52% vs 24%; eccentric and low-energy shockwave comparable
Corticosteroid injection — plantar heel pain (MA) (Whittaker 2019 BMC Musculoskelet Disord) M: similar to placebo injection short and medium term; less effective than dry needling in the longer term (SMD 1.45)
JOSPT CPG Quotes — LAS 2021, Achilles 2018 & Heel Pain 2023Koc 2023
LAS CPG — physical agents (Martin 2021 JOSPT) (Guideline): ice with exercise (C); pulsed shortwave diathermy (C); low-level laser for initial pain (C); electrotherapy conflicting (D); “Clinicians should not use ultrasound for the management of acute ankle sprains” (A)
Achilles CPG — other interventions (Martin 2018 JOSPT) (Guideline): stretching (C); iontophoresis with dexamethasone for acute presentations (B); “should not use night splints” (C); “should not use therapeutic elastic tape” (F); heel lifts, orthoses, low-level laser — no recommendation, contradictory evidence (D)
Heel pain CPG — orthoses, night splints, physical agents & scope (Koc 2023 JOSPT) (Guideline): “should not use orthoses ... as an isolated treatment for short-term pain relief” (B); orthoses combined with other treatment (C); “a 1- to 3-month program of night splints” for first-step morning pain (A) · “should not use ultrasound to enhance the benefits of stretching” (A); low-level laser as part of rehabilitation for short-term pain (B); phonophoresis with ketoprofen (C); electrotherapy second line — iontophoresis or premodulated interferential (D) · shockwave, corticosteroid and PRP injection were outside the CPG scope (scoping summary only)

Sports medicine BFR (review) (Lorenz 2021 J Athl Train) L: resistance exercise typically 20 % to 40 % of 1-RM
