A · Joint Mobilisation, Stretching, Taping & Dry Needling EvidenceMANUAL & NEEDLING
Manual Therapy, Stretching & TapingWeerasekara 2018
Joint mobilisation for ankle sprain (MA) (Weerasekara 2018 Arch Phys Med Rehabil) M: immediate benefit for posteromedial dynamic balance; short-term improvement in weight-bearing dorsiflexion (P = .003)
LAS CPG — manual therapy (acute & CAI) (Martin 2021 JOSPT) (Guideline): acute sprain: manual therapy within pain-free movement alongside exercise (A) · CAI: manual therapy incl. mobilization with movement for dorsiflexion and balance (A); external support not as a stand-alone (B)
Heel pain CPG — manual therapy, stretching & taping (Koc 2023 JOSPT) (Guideline): manual therapy to lower-extremity joints and soft tissue (A); plantar-fascia-specific and gastrocnemius / soleus stretching (A); rigid or elastic taping with other treatment for short-term pain and function (A)
Dry Needling Evidence & CPG GradesCotchett 2014
Dry needling for plantar heel pain (RCT, 1 per week × 6) (Cotchett 2014 Phys Ther) M: first-step pain −14.4 mm vs sham (below minimal important difference); NNT 4; minor transient adverse events 32%
LAS CPG — fibularis needling for CAI (Martin 2021 JOSPT) (Guideline): dry needling of the fibularis group with proprioceptive training (C)
Heel pain CPG — dry needling (Koc 2023 JOSPT) (Guideline): “dry needling to MTrP in the gastrocnemius, soleus, and plantar muscles” for short- and long-term pain (B)
B · Dry Needling Safety Box (Adverse Events, Serious Risks & Anticoagulants)NEEDLING SAFETY
Adverse Event Rates (Prospective Studies)Brady 2014
Adverse events — 7,629 treatments (Brady 2014 J Man Manip Ther) M: mild AEs 19.18 %; upper risk rate for significant AEs ≤ 0.04 %; bruising 7.55 %, bleeding 4.65 %
Adverse events — 20,464 treatments (Boyce 2020 Int J Sports Phys Ther) M: minor AE 36.7 %; major AE < .1 %; most common major: prolonged symptom aggravation .03 %, fainting .02 %, forgotten needles .01 %
Serious Risks & Anticoagulant CautionKearns 2019
Known serious risks (Halle 2016 Int J Sports Phys Ther) E: pneumothorax, cardiac tamponade, haematoma, infection, CNS injury; filament needles 32–36 gauge (.25–.20 mm)
Anticoagulants / coagulopathy (Kearns 2019 J Man Manip Ther) E: caution — possible increased incidence or severity of haematoma and bruising
