Achilles & Plantar Heel Loading + CPG QuotesBeyer 2015
Achilles tendinosis — heavy-load eccentric (12 weeks) (Alfredson 1998 Am J Sports Med) L: all 15 patients back to preinjury running
Achilles tendinopathy — HSR vs eccentric (RCT) (Beyer 2015 Am J Sports Med) H: equally good, lasting results; HSR greater satisfaction at 12 weeks
Plantar fasciitis — high-load strength (RCT) (Rathleff 2015 Scand J Med Sci Sports) M: FFI 29 points lower than stretching at 3 months; no difference at 1, 6, 12 months
Achilles CPG — exercise & activity modification (Martin 2018 JOSPT) (Guideline): “mechanical loading, which can be either in the form of eccentric exercise, or a heavy-load, slow-speed (concentric/eccentric) exercise program” (A); “exercise at least twice weekly within their pain tolerance” (F) · complete rest is not indicated — continue recreational activity within pain tolerance (B)
Heel pain CPG — resistance training & lean body mass (Koc 2023 JOSPT) (Guideline): resistance training for foot and ankle musculature (B); education on lean body mass (E)
LAS CPG Exercise / Prevention & Pain Traffic LightJOSPT 2021
LAS CPG — prevention & exercise (Martin 2021 JOSPT) (Guideline): prophylactic bracing to reduce first-time sprain (A); after a first sprain: bracing + proprioceptive and balance-focused exercise (A) · acute: “use external supports, such as braces or taping, and ... progressively bear weight” (A); structured therapeutic exercise (A) · CAI: proprioceptive / neuromuscular exercise (A)
Pain-monitoring model (Sprague 2021 Pilot Feasibility Stud · Cavaggion 2024 Open Access J Sports Med · Silbernagel 2007 Am J Sports Med): pain should not exceed 5/10 on the NPRS during or immediately after activity and should return to pre-activity levels by the following morning (E); low (0–2), acceptable (2–5) and high (5–10) risk zones were based on clinical experience (E); continued tendon loading with a pain-monitoring model showed no negative effects (M)
